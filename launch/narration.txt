Come back to a local AI chat on your Mac, and it has to reload its memory from the SSD first.
Today, Mac LLM servers load that whole cache, and only then start computing. The GPU just waits.
So we stream it. While the GPU runs one layer, the SSD is already reading the next.
Reading and computing now happen at the same time.
And on Apple Silicon, unified memory puts those bytes right where the GPU can use them. No copy step.
On an M4 MacBook, resuming a six-thousand-token chat went from point four six seconds, to point two nine. One point six times faster, with bit-identical output.
Short messages are the sweet spot. And that's exactly what AI agents send.
